# Clipboard Cleaner 1.0.1

Version **1.0.1 (2)** adds a brief confirmation after clearing the active system clipboard. Everything else is unchanged from 1.0.0.

## Features

- Open the menu bar window and click **Delete Clipboard** to clear the current system clipboard.
- The button briefly shows a green **✓ Cleared!** confirmation for about **0.8 seconds**, then returns to **Delete Clipboard**.
- The transition respects **Reduce Motion**; the confirmation and reset remain.
- **Close App**, the menu bar window, icon, privacy behavior, App Sandbox, and Hardened Runtime are unchanged.
- Universal Release build for Apple Silicon and Intel; macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Launch at login

Automatic launch remains optional and configured manually in macOS **Login Items → Open at Login** (under **Login Items & Extensions** on newer macOS versions). The app does not register a login item itself. **Close App** quits the current session.

Launching the app does not clear the clipboard. Clearing requires clicking **Delete Clipboard**.

## Privacy

The app only calls `NSPasteboard.general.clearContents()` when **Delete Clipboard** is clicked. It does not monitor, read, store, or transmit clipboard contents, keep a history, make network requests, or collect telemetry. Copies held by clipboard managers, other apps, or other devices may remain. This is not a secure-erasure guarantee.
